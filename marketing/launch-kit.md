## Store listing copy for the Little Pause 3.1.0 update

Title: **Little Pause — Website Blocker & Focus Timer**

Short description: **Pause before distracting websites. Choose gentle delays, optional voice checks, and focus sessions. No account needed.**

Full description draft:

Little Pause adds a moment to think before opening distracting websites. Choose a timed pause, an optional spoken phrase, or a block for each site. Start a 25-minute, 50-minute, or custom Focus session when you want a clearer boundary around your next task.

Previously called Talk to Unlock, Little Pause brings a simpler interface to the same project. Existing saved rules are preserved by the update.

Start small:
1. Choose the websites you want to protect.
2. Pick Take a pause, Say a phrase, or Keep blocked.
3. Open a protected site and follow the action you chose.

New users start with an empty site list. New rules default to a ten-second pause. More options include schedules, daily allowances, cooldowns, temporary unlock windows, and whether a site stays blocked during Focus. Emergency access is configurable, and bypasses are recorded locally.

Settings and activity are stored in Chrome on your device. There are no developer-operated analytics or advertising services. Voice checks use browser speech recognition; depending on the browser, speech may be processed by its service provider. Little Pause does not save audio or recognized transcripts. Voice is optional.

This extension works on supported website pages in Chrome. It cannot block native applications, other browsers, or Chrome's internal pages. It is an adjustable browsing aid, not tamper-proof parental control.
