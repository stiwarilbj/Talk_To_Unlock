Plain-language privacy

Your pauses stay close to home.

Little Pause is designed to keep your extension data in Chrome. This page explains what the extension and this public website do and do not collect.

Extension storage

Little Pause stores your settings, site rules, unlock windows, daily usage totals, Focus session state, and activity in Chrome’s storage.local area on your device. Activity is retained for the number of days selected in Settings, from 1 to 90 days, and is capped at 1,000 events. Daily usage is keyed to your computer’s local date.

Microphone and speech

A microphone stream is requested only when you choose a Voice rule’s voice control. The stream is stopped after success, failure, cancellation, or timeout. Little Pause does not save audio, recognized transcript text, or a voice profile. Chrome’s speech-recognition implementation and whether speech processing happens on-device or through a browser service depend on the browser and its settings; the extension does not control that processing.

How site access works

The content script is allowed to run on ordinary HTTP and HTTPS pages because the extension needs to notice when a page matches one of your rules. Little Pause applies a blocker only when its local settings contain a matching enabled domain rule. It does not send page content to the project, and it cannot run on browser-internal pages such as chrome://.

What the extension does not do

  • No account, login, email collection, advertising, or analytics.
  • No sale or sharing of extension settings, activity, audio, or transcripts.
  • No silent preset list on a fresh installation. You choose which sites to protect.
  • No remote database for your rules, grants, Focus sessions, or activity.

This website

This site is a static GitHub Pages project hosted at the public repository. It uses no tracking scripts, forms, accounts, or advertising. GitHub may process ordinary hosting and security logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. Little Pause does not receive those logs.
